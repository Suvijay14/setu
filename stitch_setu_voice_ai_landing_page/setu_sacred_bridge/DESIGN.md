## Brand & Style

This design system serves elderly parents and their care circles through an empathetic, voice-first digital sanctuary. The brand identity bridges generational distances and technological apprehension, translating voice interactions into a dignified, familial ritual. Rather than presenting as cold artificial intelligence, the experience evokes warmth, patience, reverence, and filial devotion.

The design movement blends **Warm Spiritual Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Layering**. Deep nocturnal backdrops anchor the interface in calm, distraction-free stillness, while glowing saffron and radiant amber accents echo the ceremonial warmth of brass lamps and sacred motifs. Interfaces prioritize extreme clarity, generous target sizes, high typographic hierarchy, and gentle glowing affordances that signal active listening without jarring motion or cognitive overload.

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for low visual fatigue and high contrast readability under diverse domestic lighting conditions.

- **Primary (`#FF9933` - Saffron Glow):** Represents guidance, auspicious voice states, primary CTAs, and active voice transcription waves. It holds spiritual and emotional resonance across Indian households.
- **Secondary (`#FDBA74` - Warm Marigold):** Softens the intense saffron for subheadings, active listening tags, metadata chips, and warm secondary contextual cues.
- **Tertiary (`#FF6B6B` - Soft Coral):** Dedicated strictly to vital health alerts, emergency calls, and medication misses, ensuring urgent legibility without inducing panic.
- **Neutral (`#0A0F1E` - Midnight Temple):** The deep navy canvas. Surface tiers (`#12192D`, `#18223C`, `#1E2B4C`) provide physical elevation and structural boundary definition without relying on stark white dividing lines.
- **Text Hierarchies:** High contrast pure white (`#FFFFFF`) ensures effortless reading for deteriorating eyesight, balanced by neutral slate (`#94A3B8`) for secondary details and warm gold (`#FDBA74`) for contextual metadata.

## Typography

Typography balances cultural gravitas with high-legibility mechanics. 

- **Playfair Display** renders core greetings, status affirmations, Hindi transliterations, and section headliners. It conveys literary presence, age-old wisdom, and warmth. Its proportions feel personal and respectful rather than corporate.
- **Inter** handles all transcription logs, conversational chat bubbles, interactive controls, and instructions. With generous x-height, neutral apertures, and rigid vertical metrics, it guarantees high legibility across Hindi phonetics and English translations.
- Scaled up base text sizes (`body-xl` and `body-lg`) ensure elderly individuals can read real-time voice prompts without squinting or needing extreme system font scaling.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model prioritizes unhurried, comfortable visual navigation using a 12-column adaptive fluid grid on desktop, 8 columns on tablet, and a focused 4-column structure on mobile devices.

- **Breathing Room:** Outer margins and gaps are scaled larger than standard apps (`1.5rem` on mobile, scaling to `3rem` on desktop) to eliminate accidental screen touches and visual claustrophobia.
- **Single-Focus Composition:** Voice-driven tasks, transcription confirmations, and emergency summaries occupy deliberate, wide horizontal expanses rather than cramped multi-column panels.
- **Touch Ergonomics:** Interactive trigger areas observe a strict minimum target footprint of 56px by 56px to ensure accessibility for trembling or low-dexterity fingertips.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on deep tonal stepping, subtle saffron radial glows, and delicate low-contrast outlines.

- **Atmospheric Tiers:** The screen base rests on `#0A0F1E`. Card containers step forward to `#12192D` (Base cards), `#18223C` (Hover/Active modules), and `#1E2B4C` (Floating speech prompts and dialogs).
- **Sacred Mandala Halos:** When the AI is listening or speaking, surfaces do not produce harsh drop shadows. Instead, they project a diffuse, warm ambient aura: `0 0 40px rgba(255, 153, 51, 0.18)` and `0 0 80px rgba(255, 153, 51, 0.08)`, referencing glowing diya lamps.
- **Low-Contrast Boundaries:** Containers utilize a 1px border colored with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` or warm saffron accents `rgba(255, 153, 51, 0.25)` rather than heavy opaque strokes. This softly delineates edges while maintaining visual serenity.

## Shapes

The design system applies a **Rounded (Level 2)** language. Base elements use `0.5rem` (8px), cards and dialogue sheets scale to `1rem` (16px), and prominent voice action modules utilize `1.5rem` (24px). 

Voice recording or listening pods utilize full circular pill forms to convey gentleness, approachability, and ease of use. Sharp edges are deliberately avoided to keep interfaces soft, welcoming, and non-threatening.

## Components

### Voice Interaction Sphere (Hero Component)
- **Visuals:** A prominent circular button (minimum 80x80px) floating at the ergonomic center-bottom, wrapped in a pulsing dual-layer gradient (`#FF9933` to `#FDBA74`).
- **States:** Idle presents a steady warm saffron glow. Active listening blooms outward with an organic radial wave mimicking subtle concentric mandala petals.

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Saffron background (`#FF9933`) with dark navy text (`#0A0F1E`) at bold weights. Includes generous padding (`1rem` vertical, `2rem` horizontal) for easy tapping.
- **Secondary:** Surface tint (`#18223C`) with warm muted border (`rgba(253, 186, 116, 0.3)`) and white text (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Emergency / Alert Action:** Coral fill (`#FF6B6B`) with white text (`#FFFFFF`) for immediate SOS dispatch.

### Speech & Conversation Cards
- Elevated on `#12192D` with `1rem` corner rounding.
- Hindi transcriptions are set in high-contrast `Playfair Display` or clean `Inter` with 1.375rem font size.
- Real-time translations or contextual family notes sit nested inside `#18223C` with warm amber highlights (`#FDBA74`).

### Chips & Status Indicators
- **Family Status & Medication Badges:** Rounded pill containers with a tinted background (`rgba(255, 153, 51, 0.12)`) and text in `#FDBA74`.
- Never rely on color alone; each badge pairs an expressive icon with clear bilingual text labels.

### Input Fields & Confirmation Sheets
- Form controls emphasize voice-to-text dictation with prominent mic triggers.
- Text inputs use dark `#12192D` backgrounds with 1.5px borders in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, switching to `#FF9933` upon focus. Placeholders are set in high-legibility slate (`#94A3B8`).
